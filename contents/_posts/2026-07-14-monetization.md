Today I added a Ko-fi widget to this site.

I have been thinking for a while about how to keep this project alive without making it too complicated or intrusive. Writing articles, maintaining the site, organizing the content, and keeping everything available online takes time and some resources. For that reason, I decided to add a simple donation option through [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/){:target="_blank"}.

This is completely optional. If you find value in the content I publish here, and you want to support the effort behind it, a small contribution is very welcome. It will help me cover part of the costs and continue improving this space.

I want to keep this blog simple and honest. I will continue sharing what I learn, and if you decide to support it, I will be truly grateful.

**Note:** to avoid losing too much of the donation to fees, I recommend using a **card**, **Google Pay**, or **Apple Pay**. But if you prefer PayPal, that is also an option.
